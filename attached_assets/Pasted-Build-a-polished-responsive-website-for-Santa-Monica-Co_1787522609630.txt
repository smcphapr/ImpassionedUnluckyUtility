Build a polished, responsive website for Santa Monica College Pre-Health Professional Association (PHPA).

This is a student-run organization for students exploring medicine, PA, nursing, dentistry, physical therapy, pharmacy, allied health, and students who are still figuring out their path.

The site should feel youthful, editorial, campus-focused, and slightly scrapbook/zine-inspired, but still clean and credible. It should NOT look like a hospital website, generic nonprofit template, corporate SaaS site, or overly cute Canva graphic.

Use this visual system:

Warm cream background: #F4F0E6
Muted navy: #18365B
Faded yellow: #D9B85F
Dusty blue: #8FA9C1
Dark ink: #202A32
Soft paper blue: #D7E0E5
Headline font style: editorial serif similar to DM Serif Display
Body/navigation: clean sans serif similar to DM Sans
Handwritten accents should be sparse and subtle

Overall look:

student publication / campus magazine
asymmetrical but organized
cream paper feel
subtle hand-drawn stars, arrows, underlines, tape, paper scraps, notebook/grid accents
real-looking empty image placeholders, not AI-generated people
no gradients
no glossy 3D
no generic medical icons
no stock stethoscope imagery
no bright clinical blue
no excessive rounded pills
no huge blocks of text

Technical requirements:

responsive on desktop and mobile
simple static site architecture
easy for future student board members to update
store editable event and opportunity content in clearly labeled data files, such as events.json, opportunities.json, and board.json
add comments in those files explaining exactly what future board members should edit
avoid unnecessary backend/database complexity
use reusable components
use semantic HTML and accessible contrast
build the full site with placeholder links where needed

Most important: make the design feel cool, student-led, editorial, warm, and memorable, not generic. Use visual restraint. The site should feel like the web version of a well-designed campus zine.
